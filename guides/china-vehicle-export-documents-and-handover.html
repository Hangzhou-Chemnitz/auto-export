Project-based review: this is not a fixed document package, shipping promise or destination-market compliance guarantee. Each project is reviewed against its actual vehicle, order structure and proposed handover scope.

1Start with the project brief

Share your company name or website, commercial role, requested model or category, key configuration requirements, intended quantity and final destination.

2Confirm the proposed handover term

State whether you want to discuss FCA, FOB or EXW. The agreed handover location, included scope and responsibilities are addressed for the actual order.

3Review vehicle-specific documentation

Commercial, transport, origin or available vehicle paperwork can be discussed once the selected vehicle, route and order structure are clear.

4Confirm local-market responsibility

The buyer or local importer confirms licensing, homologation, registration, tax and other destination-market requirements for the exact vehicle.

Information That Makes a Document Discussion Useful

Common Questions

Is the same document set available for every vehicle?

No. Availability and relevance are checked for the selected vehicle, order structure, route and destination.

Can this guide confirm a vehicle is importable in my market?

No. The buyer or local importer must confirm destination-market import, homologation, registration, tax and other requirements for the exact vehicle.

Can used cars or machinery follow the same process?

They can be reviewed as projects, but the buyer should additionally state condition, mileage and inspection preferences for used vehicles, or the required specification and intended use for machinery.
