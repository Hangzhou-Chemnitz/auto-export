China Vehicle Importer Request Checklist

Share the commercial details first, then review the relevant China-market vehicle and export handover scope for the actual project.

1. Company and commercial role
2. Vehicle, configuration and quantity
3. Destination and handover term
Project-based review: this page is not a stock list or a fixed-price offer. Each request is assessed for its specific vehicle, quantity, destination and proposed FCA, FOB or EXW handover scope.

1Start With Your Commercial Profile

Share your company name and website, or a short description of your dealership, import business or commercial procurement role. This gives the request the context needed for a project review.

2Specify the Vehicle You Need

Include the requested model or vehicle category, key configuration requirements and intended quantity. If there is more than one option, list the preferred model first and alternatives separately.

3Add Used-Car or Machinery Requirements

For used vehicles, include preferred condition, mileage range and inspection preferences. For special-purpose vehicles or machinery, include the required specification and intended project use.

4Confirm Destination and Handover Preference

State the final destination city and country, plus the FCA, FOB or EXW term you want to discuss. This lets the project be reviewed against the actual handover scope.

What a Complete Request Should Include

  • Company name, website or commercial role
  • Requested model or vehicle category
  • Key configuration requirements
  • Intended quantity
  • Final destination city and country
  • Preferred FCA, FOB or EXW term

Before You Submit

Who confirms local import, homologation and registration requirements?

The buyer or local importer remains responsible for confirming import licensing, homologation, registration, tax and other destination-market requirements for the exact vehicle.

Does a request confirm vehicle availability or a fixed price?

No. Availability, vehicle condition, configuration, price and document scope are confirmed only for the specific request.

Do FCA, FOB and EXW include the same services?

No. The agreed handover location, included services, responsibilities and items outside scope are discussed for the specific project.
