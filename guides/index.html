02 / DIRECT ANSWERS FOR DEALERS & IMPORTERS

Frequently asked B2B sourcing questions.

What changes on April 1, 2027 for China New Energy Vehicle (NEV) exports?

Under Ministry of Finance & State Taxation Administration Announcement 2026 No. 29 (issued October 12, 2026), the VAT export tax rebate rate for 67 eligible NEV tariff lines (including specified 8703 battery-electric and plug-in hybrid passenger vehicles) decreases from 13% to 9% starting April 1, 2027. Eligibility for the 13% rate depends strictly on the Export Date printed on the China Customs Export Declaration.

How does China's 180-day used-car export rule work?

Under Notice Shang Mao Han [2025] No. 648 (effective January 1, 2026), exporting a Chinese-registered vehicle within 180 days (inclusive) of its initial registration date requires an OEM-stamped After-Sales Maintenance Service Confirmation Letter. Genuine used vehicles registered for more than 180 days follow the standard used-car export licence review using original Chinese registration certificates matched to the 17-digit VIN.

Can my appointed freight forwarder inspect the car in China before I wire funds?

Yes. When a specific vehicle is physically in stock and available, your appointed forwarder in China can visit the warehouse to inspect the car, doorjamb nameplate, and 17-digit VIN even before you pay a deposit (note: inspection alone does not hold stock; reservation starts upon deposit receipt). For order-sourced vehicles, full forwarder VIN and condition inspection is supported at the agreed China handover point before balance settlement.

Which trade terms, handover ports, and trial order sizes are supported?

Commercial dealers can begin with a 1-unit trial order under EXW, FCA, or FOB terms at the agreed China port — including Khorgos, Kashgar, Nansha, Ningbo, Shanghai, Tianjin, or Qingdao. Each quotation is built specifically for the chosen handover port and confirmed configuration.
