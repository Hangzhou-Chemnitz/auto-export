China NEV Export VAT Rebate Drops 13% → 9% on April 1, 2027: What Overseas Dealers Need to Know

Based on Ministry of Finance (MOF) & State Taxation Administration (STA) Announcement 2026 No. 29 (财政部 税务总局公告2026年第29号), published October 12, 2026.

Key Statutory Rule in One Sentence: Starting April 1, 2027, China's VAT export tax rebate rate on 67 New Energy Vehicle (NEV) tariff lines (covering specified 8703 EVs and PHEVs) decreases from 13% to 9%, determined strictly by the Export Date (出口日期) shown on the China Customs Export Declaration.

1. Official Primary Source & Scope

On October 12, 2026, China's Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration jointly issued Announcement 2026 No. 29 on official central portals (chinatax.gov.cn and mof.gov.cn). Separately, under Announcement 2026 No. 2 (issued January 8, 2026), standalone battery product VAT export rebates drop from 6% to 0% on January 1, 2027.

Decisive Cut-Off Timestamp Strictly the Export Date (出口日期) recorded on the formal China Customs Export Declaration — not the proforma invoice date, deposit transfer date, or inland dispatch date.

2. Why the Customs "Export Date" Matters for Q1 2027 Procurement

Many overseas buyers assume signing a sales contract in March locks in pre-April China-side tax treatment. In practice, factory allocation, export licence issuance, inland carrier transit to Khorgos, Kashgar, or Chinese seaports, and border customs clearance take real calendar time:

  • Pre-clearance draft vs. formal exit date: When a customs declaration is first transmitted for port handover, the Export Date field remains blank until actual customs exit is recorded. Only declarations bearing an official Export Date on or before March 31, 2027 fall under the pre-April window.
  • Export pathway distinction: Not every China export order uses the same tax structure (for example, OEM direct China-port packages, third-party licensed export channels, and self-operated export declarations settle tax differently). Always confirm with your supplier before deposit how an April border crossover affects your specific quotation.
  • Buffer planning for Q1 2027: Commercial dealers ordering factory-allocated EVs or PHEVs for Q1 shipment should confirm realistic production, export licence, and inland transit lead times well ahead of March 2027.
