China Vehicle Export Pathways (2026–2027) & Pre-Payment 17-Digit VIN Verification

How overseas auto dealers verify the exact vehicle, export licence route, and China handover stage before transferring deposit or balance funds.

1. Two Distinct Legal Export Pathways in China (2026–2027)

Since January 1, 2026, two central Chinese regulations define how passenger vehicles obtain export licences: Notice Shang Mao Han [2025] No. 648 (used-vehicle registration timing) and Joint Announcement 2025 No. 54 (battery-electric passenger car export licensing under HS reference 8703801090). Understanding which pathway applies to your order prevents port delays:

Pathway Applicable Vehicles Core China Export Licence Basis
Pathway A: New-Vehicle / OEM-Backed Export Channel Brand-new factory units or vehicles exported under manufacturer / OEM-authorized enterprise channels Processed under new-vehicle export licensing or OEM-backed export channels (for example, MOFCOM 2027 public-notice Annex 1 Row 997 pairs Shandong Zhongrui with Hangzhou Chemnitz, subject to formal final announcement and per-order route confirmation; note that shared OEM confirmation excludes aftermarket modifications).
Pathway B: Genuine >180-Day Registered Used Vehicles Chinese-registered passenger cars where more than 180 calendar days have elapsed since initial registration Under Shang Mao Han [2025] No. 648, vehicles registered ≤180 days require an OEM-stamped after-sales confirmation letter, whereas genuine >180-day registered used cars apply via used-vehicle export licensing with original Chinese registration book (绿本) matched to the 17-digit VIN.

2. Four-Stage Verification Protocol: How First-Time Dealers Eliminate Payment Risk

The #1 concern of any overseas dealer working with a new Chinese supplier is simple: "How do I know the car exists and that the exact VIN I inspected is the car handed over at the port?" Hangzhou Chemnitz separates every transaction into verifiable physical milestones:

Stage 1 — Stock vs. Order-Sourced Transparency (Before Contracting) If a unit is physically in stock and unreserved, we share its actual 17-digit VIN upfront, and your appointed freight forwarder in China is welcome to inspect the physical car at the warehouse at any stage — even before you wire the deposit. (Inspection alone does not hold the car; stock reservation locks upon deposit receipt.) If a trim/color must be factory-ordered or sourced, we state that clearly upfront, issue the contract first, and append the 17-digit VIN once assigned.
Stage 2 — Continuous One-Take VIN & Condition Check (Before Balance Payment) Before final vehicle payment and pickup, your appointed forwarder can inspect the physical vehicle at the agreed China warehouse or border yard — checking exterior paint, interior trim, odometer reading, doorjamb nameplate, and chassis 17-digit VIN stamping. Any minor cosmetic flaw is communicated transparently for buyer confirmation before release.
Stage 3 — Forwarder Handover vs. China Export Customs Declaration Upon confirmed vehicle settlement, your appointed forwarder collects the verified vehicle at the agreed handover point (EXW, FCA, or FOB Khorgos, Kashgar, Nansha, Ningbo, Shanghai, etc.), and China-side export customs declaration is completed and transmitted to your forwarder for border exit. Note that contracting seller entity, export licence holder, and customs consignor may be distinct entities depending on the agreed route and are always shown transparently on originals.
Stage 4 — Actual Border Exit vs. Destination Import Clearance A pre-clearance customs sheet without an Export Date only reflects declaration filing; actual exit from China is evidenced later when customs records the stamped Export Date. Cross-border transit beyond the agreed China handover point and destination customs clearance/homologation are handled by the buyer's forwarder and local customs broker.
