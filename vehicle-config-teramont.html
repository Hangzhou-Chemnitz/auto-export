What is needed for a Teramont Pro quotation?

Send the configuration name or sheet, preferred exterior and interior colors, quantity and destination to our sales team. Specify the agreed Chinese collection point or port and whether you need an EXW or FOB quotation. We confirm sourcing availability and the services included before preparing the offer.

Does the Chinese guide price include shipping?

No. The manufacturer guide price is a comparison reference. The written export quotation identifies the vehicle procurement price and agreed preparation, document and logistics costs. Do not treat it as a landed price in your country; confirm destination import eligibility and local costs before ordering.
