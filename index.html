USED-VEHICLE EXPORT

Registration timing and application documents

Issued 11 Nov 2025 · Requirement applies from 1 Jan 2026

Vehicles within 180 days of registration, including day 180, require manufacturer after-sales confirmation for export applications. Exceeding this period does not automatically establish export eligibility.
