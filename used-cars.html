Before a quotation

What we confirm for a specific vehicle

Used-car export is vehicle-specific. The quotation stage is where details are checked with the source, documents and route in view.

  • Current availability and source-side vehicle information
  • Condition evidence and mileage information supplied for review
  • Registration, ownership and core export documentation path
  • Destination country, port and buyer-side import requirements provided by the buyer
  • EXW, FCA or FOB term and shipment method for the confirmed vehicle

Why request instead of browsing a static stock list?

Availability, condition, route and export eligibility can change. A short request lets us return options that match the actual destination and commercial need.
