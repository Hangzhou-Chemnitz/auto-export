Our focus remains the same as on our original website: China-market vehicle sourcing, clear configuration information and coordinated delivery.

2026 automobile export-licence eligibility list

Hangzhou Chemnitz International Trade Co., Ltd. is listed as an authorised export trading enterprise in the 2026 automobile export-licence eligibility list published under MOFCOM Announcement No. 85 of 2025. The corresponding manufacturers are 浙江淼盛汽车有限公司 (Zhejiang Miaosheng) and 众锐工设（山东）房车制造有限公司 (Zhongrui).
