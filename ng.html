FAQ

Questions from Nigerian importers

What should be confirmed before a Nigeria vehicle order?

The Nigerian importer or local agent confirms its importer status, the exact configuration and VIN acceptance when available, and the customs, conformity, registration and tax requirements that apply to the particular project before order or payment.

Can you supply used cars to Nigeria?

Used cars are not presented as the default route on this page. A specific used unit is reviewed only after the importer or local agent provides written confirmation of the rules that apply and acceptance of that unit.

Do you quote FCA, FOB or EXW?

Yes. The proforma invoice identifies the China handover point or port, included services and each party's responsibilities. Costs outside China are included only when expressly stated.

Is a website quotation a fixed offer?

No. Any commercial offer depends on the exact model, configuration, quantity, actual source and agreed China handover point at the time of review.
