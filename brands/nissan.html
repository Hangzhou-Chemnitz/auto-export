04 / SOURCING & EXPORT FAQ

Nissan · Common questions

Which Nissan models can I source from China?

Our current catalogue lists 7 models: Teana, N7, N6, Qashqai, X-Trail, NX8, Sylphy. Open each model for its China-market versions and configuration records. Catalogue counts are not stock quantities.

Which version differences should I check before choosing Nissan?

The current X-Trail sheet covers the Rongyu series, with front-wheel drive and XTRONIC CVT on both listed trims. Qashqai also refers to the Rongyu series. N6 and range-extended NX8 list engine and motor figures separately; adding them does not establish total system output.

Is a China-market Nissan the same as the version sold locally?

Not necessarily. Model year, powertrain, equipment, steering position, infotainment language and connected services may differ. Match the exact version before ordering, and have the destination importer confirm import, registration and operating requirements.

How do I get an export price for Nissan?

Send the model, trim, quantity, colour and destination country or port, and state whether you want EXW, FOB or another quotation basis. Our team will confirm the vehicle price and cost scope. Freight, insurance and destination taxes are included only where itemised in the written quote.

Is stock available, and what is the delivery timing?

Stock is checked for the chosen version, colour and quantity. Delivery timing is around 15 days, subject to vehicle readiness and carrier schedules; this is not a guaranteed arrival or customs-clearance time for every destination.

What are the payment split and export documents?

Payment is 30% deposit and 70% balance. Documents include the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, export licence and certificate of conformity. Confirm order milestones and any additional destination documents when placing the order.

How do I confirm warranty and parts after export?

Tell us the country of use and exact version so the team can confirm the applicable warranty scope, service provider and parts requirements for your order. Do not assume that domestic-China warranties or local dealer policies automatically cover a cross-border purchase.
