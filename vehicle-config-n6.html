Nissan N6 China-market specifications and export project discussion

The Nissan N6 is a China-market plug-in hybrid sedan. Dongfeng Nissan lists a 1.5L PHEV engine, a 155 kW / 320 N m e-motor, a 20.3 or 21.1 kWh LFP battery and 170 or 180 km CLTC electric range across the current reference variants. The exact trim, equipment and current guide price must be checked for the requested vehicle.

See the Dongfeng Nissan China N6 specification table for the source configuration reference. A Chemnitz quotation is prepared after the selected trim, order quantity, final destination and preferred EXW, FCA or FOB term are confirmed. The importer should verify destination-market admission, homologation and registration requirements for the exact vehicle before order placement.
